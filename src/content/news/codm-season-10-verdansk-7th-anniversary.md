Verdansk is coming to Call of Duty: Mobile. The map will arrive in Battle Royale when Season 10: 7th Anniversary goes live on Wednesday, November 11 at 4 PM PT, which is 12 AM UTC on November 12.

It is the first time the Warzone map has been playable in CODM. Activision says the mobile version has been built for CODM rather than ported directly, with more than 15 major points of interest and a visual style that mixes the original map with the game's brighter look. Familiar locations such as Prison, Superstore, and Train Station are part of the package.

The map is the centrepiece of the anniversary season, but it is not the only addition. Activision has confirmed Verdansk-linked event chapters and an earnable Ghost – Desert Ghost operator skin. The seasonal events and their claim conditions will appear in the Event Center after launch.

CODM will also give the map an early airing. A creator showcase is scheduled for November 4, with seven teams playing five Battle Royale rounds on Verdansk. The event will be broadcast on YouTube, TikTok, and in-game.

For players who know Verdansk from Warzone, the layout will be familiar. The tactics will not transfer cleanly. CODM has its own movement, classes, vehicles, loot pacing, and touch controls, all of which change how rotations and late-zone fights play out. The practical advice is to treat the map as new: learn the hot drops, the vehicle routes, and the edges of the safe zone before worrying about old Warzone habits.

Season 10 also marks seven years of CODM. The anniversary branding runs through the season, and Verdansk is the headline addition rather than a side event. The update lands at 4 PM PT on November 11; players in UTC+5:30 can expect it in the early hours of November 12.
