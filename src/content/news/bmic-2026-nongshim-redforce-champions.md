Nongshim RedForce won BMIC 2026 by 82 points, turning a final that had been decided by a single point for two days into one of the most lopsided finishes in the tournament's short history.

The Korean side closed on 233 points at The Dome, SVP Indoor Stadium in Mumbai, with DRX second on 151 and Dplus KIA third on 149. Nongshim finished with five chicken dinners and 148 finishes across 18 matches, both tournament highs.

The title race had been tight for most of the weekend. Nongshim led Dplus KIA 72–71 after Day 1 and 100–99 after Day 2. On the final day, Nongshim scored 133 points across six matches, won three of them, and added 91 finishes to end the argument.

The reward is ₹30 lakh from the ₹1 crore prize pool and a place in the PMGC 2026 Gauntlet stage. DRX, the defending champions, take ₹15 lakh and the PMGC Group Stage slot that came with their PUBG Mobile Pro Series placing. Dplus KIA earned ₹10 lakh and also advance to the Group Stage, while Japan's Valor took the other Group Stage place.

India's best finish came from Gladiators Esports, who closed with a 66-point final day and two chicken dinners to take fourth on 144. Outrage Esports finished fifth on 125 and K9 Esports sixth on 116, giving the host country three teams in the top six.

The individual honours were split. K9's Taurus was named Best Clutch, while Nongshim swept the rest: XZY1 took both the Google Play MVP and Grenadier awards, and SOEZ was named Best IGL.

BMIC 2026 ran from October 30 to November 1 with 16 teams from India, South Korea, and Japan playing a single 18-match lobby. Six teams came from India, five from South Korea, and five from Japan. The top two earned PMGC 2026 places, with the champion upgraded to the Gauntlet stage.

For Nongshim, the win adds to a season that already included the Korea Esports League 2026 title. For the Indian scene, the final standings show both the size of the gap on the last day and how close the chase has become: Gladiators missed a PMGC place by seven points despite winning as many chicken dinners as third-placed Dplus KIA.
