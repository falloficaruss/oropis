Nongshim RedForce won BMIC 2026 as a team, and the individual awards followed the trophy. XZY1 took both the Google Play MVP and Grenadier awards, while SOEZ was named Best IGL. K9 Esports' Taurus was the only Indian player to take an individual honour, winning Best Clutch.

Taurus, known as The Eliminator after his run at BMSD, won the clutch award against a field that included the best of Korea and Japan. K9 finished sixth overall on 116 points, one place behind Outrage Esports, as India took fourth through sixth.

XZY1's double came in a title run that ended with Nongshim on 233 points, 82 clear of DRX. The team finished with five chicken dinners and 148 finishes, both tournament highs, and closed with 133 points on the final day. The MVP and Grenadier awards reflect the same aggression that produced those numbers.

SOEZ, who moved from DRX to Nongshim with Hyunbin, was the IGL behind the final-day break. Nongshim had been level with Dplus KIA for two days before scoring 91 finishes across the last six matches and winning three of them.

The awards cap a season in which Nongshim also won the Korea Esports League 2026. For K9 and Taurus, the Best Clutch award is a consolation for a campaign that ended one place outside the Indian top four but with a piece of individual silverware.
