Nongshim RedForce won BMIC 2026 in Mumbai on November 1. After a 12-hour trip home to South Korea, Hyunbin went straight back to PUBG Mobile.

NS RedForce head coach Janchi shared a photo on his Instagram story of Hyunbin at his desk in the team house, phone in hand. "12 hours from Mumbai to our team house in Korea and Hyunbin is already gaming," Janchi wrote. "He said he was just going to set up his gear. This guy is a different breed."

Hyunbin is Jeon Hyun-bin, a South Korean player who joined NS RedForce on January 22, 2026. Before that he spent about a year and a half at DRX, having moved there in June 2024 after six months at DUKSAN Esports. He was the MVP of BMIC 2025 in Delhi, where DRX won the title and he recorded 44 finishes across 18 matches. He then won PMPS 2026 Season 1 and its MVP award with NS RedForce in May.

He arrived in Mumbai as the defending BMIC MVP in a different jersey and left with the trophy again. NS RedForce produced one of the most one-sided final days in BMIC's short history: after two days level with Dplus KIA at 100 and 99 points, they scored 133 points across the last six matches, won three of them, and finished 82 points clear. Hyunbin's teammates took the individual awards this time, with XZY1 named MVP and Grenadier and SOEZ named Best IGL.

The win sent NS RedForce to the Gauntlet stage of PMGC 2026 in Türkiye, where the prize pool is $3 million. DRX also advanced to the PMGC Group Stage, while Team SouL qualified directly as BGMI Showdown 2026 champions. Dates for PMGC 2026 have not been confirmed.

Hyunbin finished sixth at PMGC 2024 and PMGC 2025 with DRX. He now heads to the world championship with a team that has already won PMPS 2026 Season 1 and BMIC 2026 this year.

The team-house detail is small, but it explains the result. When the desk, the bedroom, and the teammates are in the same place, the gap between travelling and training gets very short. Hyunbin's first night home was also his first night back at work.
