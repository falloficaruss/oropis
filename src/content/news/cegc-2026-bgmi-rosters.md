The Chennai Esports Global Championship 2026 has 32 BGMI teams chasing 16 Grand Finals places and a share of ₹75 lakh, and the registration sheet says as much about the off-season as it does about the group stage.

The headline move is Shadow's return to Revenant XSpark. He is registered as the IGL, with Phoenix, Pain, and Trace in the main four. NinjaJOD, the team's IGL at BMSD 2026, is listed as a substitute fragger, and analyst Chirag is also on the bench.

Sukuna, who left Revenant XSpark in the latest shake-up, is now a substitute at Meta Ninza, the team Shadow came from. ProToN is with iQOO Team Tamilas, Punkk captains VR1 Esports, and JDGaming is a substitute filter IGL at Team Outrage.

Nebula won BGMS Season 5 in September but its CEGC lineup is completely different: OWAIS is the IGL, with MAFIAA, VENOM, and ScaryJoD. AADIII and KNOWME, names that match Nebula's BGMS IGL Aadi and Finals MVP KnowMe, are registered together under Vasista Esports.

K9 Esports brings Taurus06, who won Best Clutch at BMIC 2026 in Mumbai. Gladiators, India's best team at BMIC in fourth, kept Aquanox as filter IGL.

The format is unforgiving. The top eight from Group Stage 1 go straight to the Upper Bracket. The other 24 play Group Stage 2, a round robin, where the top eight join the Upper Bracket and the bottom 16 drop to the Lower Bracket. The Upper Bracket's top eight qualify for the Grand Finals. The rest of the field comes through the Last Chance Qualifier, which takes the bottom eight from the Upper Bracket and the top eight from the Lower Bracket.

Group Stage 1 runs four matches a day: Rondo, Erangel, Erangel, and Miramar. Group A starts at 1 PM IST and Group B at 4 PM IST. Matches stream on Skyesports' Kick channel.

The roster sheet also shows a clear style trend. Assaulters outnumber supports almost three to one, and 14 of the 32 teams registered no support at all. Team AX, WELT Esports, and Team Hector listed every non-IGL player as an assaulter, while Blitz Esports and Lastade Esports went all-in on fraggers. Only six teams named a filter IGL: Gladiators, Meta Ninza, Team Outrage, Raven, 4TR Official, and GoSolo Higgboson.

That is a lot of firepower and very little cover. The group stage will show which teams can turn it into points.
