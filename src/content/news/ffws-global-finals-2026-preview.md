Free Fire's world championship has a new shape. The FFWS Global Finals 2026 opens on November 6 with 24 teams, up from 18, and runs through November 28 at BITEC LIVE in Bangkok.

The field is split into four groups of six for Knockout Stage Phase 1. Each team plays 36 games across two weeks, with two sessions a day on match days at 2 PM and 7 PM UTC+7. The top 20 teams advance; the bottom four are eliminated.

Phase 2 runs from November 19 to 22. Twenty teams play six games a day, and the top two on each matchday book a Grand Finals place, filling eight of the 12 slots. The remaining teams get one more route through the Last Chance on November 27: 12 teams, 10 games, top four advance.

The Grand Finals on November 28 use Champion Rush. A team must reach at least 110 points to become eligible, and from the next game onward, an eligible team that wins a Booyah takes the title.

The prize pool is $1.2 million, with $350,000 for the champion, $160,000 for second, and $75,000 for third. MVP of the Day awards are worth $750 per session, and the Finals MVP takes $5,000.

India has three teams in the field: Team Apex Gaming, Resurrection, and TSG Pros. They are spread across the groups, so there will be no all-Indian lobby in Phase 1. The opening session on November 6 puts Groups A and B together at 2 PM UTC+7.

The groups are:

- Group A: ONIC, Stellar Esports, Total Winner, Buriram United Esports, Gun Dynasty, TSG Pros
- Group B: RRQ Kazu, GOW Esports, Horaa Esports, Blue Cheese, LOUD Snickers, XPROJEKT Esports
- Group C: Bigetron by Vitality, Pirate Esports, Lyon, Fluxo W7M, Resurrection, P Esports
- Group D: Team Infinity, LOS, Team Flash, All Gamers Global, Paradox Gaming, Team Apex Gaming

The expanded field puts more weight on the group phase and makes the top-20 cut the first real pressure point. A slow opening week can still be rescued, but the teams that bank points early control their path to Bangkok.
