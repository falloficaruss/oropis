Free Fire is taking its anime adaptation to one of Latin America's biggest pop-culture events. Free Fire: Daybreak will premiere its first episode at CCXP26 in São Paulo on December 5, 2026, ahead of a global release in spring 2027.

The series is produced by Garena with KADOKAWA and animated by studio Candy Box. Ken Takahashi directs. The story follows Kelly, Hayato, and Moco in New Dawn, a futuristic city controlled by the Horizon Corporation. Kelly gets caught in a conspiracy and discovers a mysterious new power.

The project was first announced in May 2024, with a first look in 2025 and a spring 2027 launch window confirmed in July 2026. The CCXP26 panel will give fans a closer look at production, and DJ Alok will join the discussion to talk about his appearance in the first episode and his contribution to the music. Alok has been a playable Free Fire character since 2019.

CCXP26 runs from December 3 to 6 at São Paulo Expo. The Free Fire booth will host Daybreak activities throughout the event, alongside the premiere and panel.

For Garena, the anime is a brand play as much as a content play. Free Fire's universe already supports collaborations and character-driven events; a scripted series gives those characters a life outside the game and a reason for fans to gather around the IP between seasons. The spring 2027 release will test whether that audience follows.
